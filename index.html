A bit about me

Thoughtful by nature.
Practical by design.

I work across strategy, interface design and front-end development. The best part is getting close to a problem, asking better questions, and making something clear enough to feel effortless.

Over the last 6 years, I’ve partnered with early-stage teams and established brands to bring useful ideas into the world. Good work is a team sport; I like working with people who care about the details and keep a sense of humour.
